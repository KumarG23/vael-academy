## Completion and compatibility

Campaign appends a `report_card_deliveries` entry `{card_index, delivered: false}` at the same time it locks each new card. The index identifies immutable history; delivery never assumes the last card. Castle date refresh (including entry after a battle or load) reserves pending cards in history order, ahead of the existing festival/feast. Reservations live only in that castle scene and suppress duplicate refreshes/callbacks.

The ceremony's own completion callback synchronously adds the shipped rank gold, attempts the S seal, unlocks the merchant and marks its receipt delivered. Before completion it has paid nothing. Missing/unplayable dialogue leaves the receipt pending and holds the following set-piece; leaving the scene mid-ceremony retries the whole ceremony on re-entry. Leaving after completion never pays or presents that card again. Festival/feast flags and festival cooking/conduct effects similarly commit only on their own completion, with separate scene-local queue reservations. This changes bookkeeping timing while preserving the ordinary player interaction, text and rewards.

Full Kai inventory retains the existing behavior: the S card pays gold and completes, but grants **no seal**, with no retry, storage or compensation. The reward line includes the seal only when space is available (including reservations for earlier queued S cards).

Ordinary saves include receipts with campaign progression, inventory and flags. A save made before completion restores pending delivery; a save made after completion restores the delivered receipt and reward together. This introduces no autosave, mid-dialogue resume or crash durability guarantee. Unsaved progress still follows the existing save architecture.

Omitted receipt metadata defaults to an empty collection: historical cards stay locked, are not regraded, replayed or automatically compensated. Future closes append new receipts normally. Save version remains 1. The untrusted restore boundary validates the entire new collection, unique integral in-range indices, boolean completion values and referenced card fields **before** touching live campaign, roster, RNG or staged battle state. Unknown fields remain tolerated; historical cards without receipts retain their prior compatibility behavior. No real player saves are rewritten.
