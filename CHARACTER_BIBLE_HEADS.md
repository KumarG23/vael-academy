# CHARACTER_BIBLE_HEADS.md — The Three Heirs

> **THEME (locked 2026-07-09):** *Belonging isn't given by blood — it's built by what you protect.*


Companion to `WORLD_BIBLE.md`, `ACADEMY_BIBLE.md`, `CAMPAIGN_STRUCTURE.md`. The three Great House heirs — the famous names every student knows before arriving (`WORLD_BIBLE.md` §2). Each embodies their house's founding Warder line (fight / hold / study) and their region, and each meets Kai as a stranger in the prologue raid before anyone knows who anyone is.

**Structural note:** whichever house the player picks, that heir is a *housemate and ally*; the other two lead the rival forces at the inter-house battles. All three must read as sympathetic rivals, never villains — they're future lords of the same kingdom.

**Romance:** all students are romanceable regardless of gender — heirs included, a pool of 18 (3 heirs + 15 house students); the per-character notes (here and in the house bibles) describe the *tone* of each romance, not whether it's available. The three heirs are the marquee arcs, set at **two female (Serra, Rina) and one male (Perrin)**. Keep everything at genre level — crushes, confessions — cast is 15–18.

---

## Perrin — Heir of Emberwing
*Crimson & gold · Firebird · the southern reach · swords / cavalry / offense (STR/SPD)*

- **Role:** cavalry — mounted, fast, hard-hitting. The house's charge embodied.
- **Lineage:** eldest child of Emberwing's ruling family, heir to the sun-drenched south. Grew up beloved, expected to lead, and genuinely suited to it — the rare golden child who isn't insufferable about it.
- **Personality:** bold, warm, loud, heart fully on his sleeve — Emberwing's culture in one person. Thrilled rather than threatened by a scrappy commoner who awakened magic; to Perrin, Kai is the best story to walk into Vael in a decade, and he says so to Kai's face. Generous to a fault, terrible at hiding his feelings, charges in first and thinks second — a mirror of Kai's own recklessness, which is why they either click instantly or spark.
- **Flaw:** privilege he's never had to examine. Means every word of his kindness and still cannot see the walls Kai has spent his life walking around. His arc is learning to.
- **With Kai:** the easiest friendship in the game if the player leans in, and a real one — but Perrin also has to *earn* it past the surface, because Kai has met charming nobles before. Strong romance candidate (heir/commoner, the classic; plays as either the warm rival-to-love or the golden boy who chose you).
- **As a rival (if unpicked):** leads Emberwing's cavalry charge at the inter-house battles — reckless, aggressive, always overcommits, and grins the whole time. Yields with a laugh and zero hard feelings.

## Serra — Heir of Tidemark
*Deep blue & silver · Leviathan · the coast · lances / armor / faith (DEF/HP)*

*(Already the built Mock Battle boss — WO-4.1. This is her as a full person, consistent with that fort-holding, disciplined role.)*

- **Role:** armored lance — holds the line, immovable, the fort-holder the current chapter already uses.
- **Lineage:** heir of Tidemark, the coastal ward-keepers — the line that *held*, literally kept the wards. Duty isn't a value she was taught; it's the water she swims in. Her family's whole identity is standing your ground so others don't fall.
- **Personality:** disciplined, chivalric, formal — reads cold at first, and it's armor over something warmer she was raised not to show. Takes everything seriously because in her family, the thing you don't take seriously is the thing that kills people. Sizes Kai up in the prologue not out of snobbery but threat-assessment — an unknown magic is exactly the kind of unquantified risk her whole upbringing screams about.
- **Flaw:** rigidity. Mistakes the rulebook for the goal. When doctrine and the right thing diverge — and the campaign will make them diverge — she's the one who has to choose, and choosing costs her.
- **With Kai:** the slowest, most-earned bond. She starts by treating him as a variable to be managed; the arc is her coming to see the person, and being quietly changed by someone who does the right thing on instinct without any rulebook at all. Romance candidate — the thaw is the whole appeal; her walls come down a brick at a time.
- **As a rival (if unpicked):** the boss the game already has — holds the fort, never chases, punishes overextension. Yields with grave respect, not rancor.

## Rina — Heir of Thornrook
*Forest green & bronze · Stag · the western wilds · bows / reason magic / technique (SKL/RES)*

- **Role:** reason mage (or mage-archer flex) — ranged, precise, cerebral. Thornrook's mind on the field.
- **Lineage:** heir of Thornrook, the line that *watched* — archivists and theorists, keepers of the western libraries. Raised among the kingdom's oldest records, which means she is, without fully realizing it, closer to the truth about the **First Crown and the silent fourth line** than almost anyone alive (`WORLD_BIBLE.md` §3–4). Doesn't have the answer — but has read the questions, and clocks Kai's mark against them the instant she sees it.
- **Personality:** cunning, curious, dry — watches before speaking, and when she speaks it's usually three moves ahead. Fascinated by Kai from the first flicker of that mark in the prologue; the danger is that fascination reads as *treating him like a specimen*, and Kai clocks it immediately. Her growth is learning the difference between studying someone and knowing them.
- **Flaw:** detachment. Curiosity outruns warmth; she'll analyze a crisis a beat longer than she should before *feeling* it. Pragmatism can tip into cold if unchecked.
- **With Kai:** intellectually magnetic and personally guarded — two people who both keep the world at arm's length, circling. If they connect, it's the meeting of the game's two sharpest minds. One of the two female marquee romances. The pull is being truly *understood*, which is exactly what Kai both craves and fears.
- **As a rival (if unpicked):** leads from range at the inter-house battles — never where you expect, always where it hurts, turns terrain into a weapon. Yields the instant the math turns against her; no ego in it.
