# CHARACTER_BIBLE_THORNROOK.md — House Thornrook

*Forest green & bronze · Stag · the western wilds · bows / reason magic · technique growths (SKL/RES)*
*Culture: cunning, scholarly, pragmatic. Understand it before you touch it.*

Companion to the other bibles. All-new roster (Thornrook had only Rina in the build). Rina (heir) is in `CHARACTER_BIBLE_HEADS.md`. Roster coverage: archer (Thessaly), mage (Corvin), scout/mobility (Marlow), research-healer (Vesper), and a martial outlier (Rurik) so the house isn't all soft-spoken intellect — full spread within the bow/reason/technique idiom.

---

## Thessaly — Archer / Sniper
*bow · high SKL · patient precision*

- **Role:** Thornrook's marksman — long reach, deadly accuracy, the house's technique growths made literal.
- **Personality:** quiet to the point of unnerving. Watches everyone, says almost nothing, and when she finally speaks it lands because it's always *true*. Not shy — deliberate. Reads a room like a range card.
- **Depth:** the silence is grief-shaped — something happened before Vael she doesn't discuss, and her stillness is control, not calm. Kai, who also arrived carrying a thing he can't explain, is someone she doesn't feel the need to perform *okayness* for.
- **With Kai:** two guarded people whose bond is built on comfortable silence and rare, real sentences. Romance candidate (the still-waters slow-burn; the payoff is her choosing to *speak*).

## Corvin — Reason Mage / Scholar
*tome · magic · bookish theorist*

- **Role:** Thornrook's core mage — ranged reason magic, delighted to explain the theory at you.
- **Personality:** bright, chatty, genuinely brilliant, and *thrilled* Kai exists — possibly the only person at Vael more excited about Kai's magic than Kai is horrified by it. Means no harm and is, at first, exactly the "treated like a specimen" problem in a friendly package.
- **Depth:** the enthusiasm hides loneliness — Corvin's brain has always outrun his peers, and he's more comfortable with ideas than people. His arc is learning that Kai is a *friend*, not the most interesting research subject to ever walk in, and the moment he chooses the person over the puzzle is his best beat.
- **With Kai:** the specimen-to-friend arc, done warmly. Romance-optional (endearing, over-eager, has to learn to see the person — then does, wholeheartedly).

## Marlow — Scout / Thief-type *(the "cunning as street-smarts" one)*
*light blade / bow · fast · utility (steal/unlock flavor)*

- **Role:** Thornrook's mobility — fast, sneaky, opens the map up. Cunning as *practical* wit, not book-learning.
- **Personality:** easygoing, sharp-eyed, a little roguish — from a *newly-ennobled* house, merchant money that bought a title a generation ago, which the old blood never lets him forget. Not a commoner — but the lowest, newest rung of nobility, sneered at as "new money." Among Thornrook's ancient scholarly families, Marlow knows exactly what it is to be looked down on, which is the nearest thing at Vael to Kai's world (and he's the first to say it's *not* the same — he'd never claim Kai's struggle as his own).
- **Depth:** hides real ambition under the casual charm — knows exactly how the kingdom's class ladder works because his family's been climbing it his whole life, and sees in Kai both a kindred outsider and, uncomfortably, an opportunity. The arc is loyalty winning out over angle.
- **With Kai:** the "fellow outsider" bond — real, but with Marlow the first to admit new-money noble and true commoner aren't the same thing — plus a live edge of will-he-actually-be-straight-with-me. Romance candidate (charming, guarded, worth the trust once earned).

## Vesper — Research Healer *(the house's healer — faith's opposite number)*
*staff · healing-as-applied-science · Thornrook's required healer*

- **Role:** Thornrook's healer, doing Tidemark-Elowen's exact combat job from the opposite worldview — healing as *research*, not faith.
- **Personality:** curious, precise, a little too fascinated by wounds — treats every injury as an interesting problem and will absolutely narrate the diagnosis while you're bleeding. Kind underneath, but the bedside manner needs work.
- **Depth:** the clinical distance is a shield; Vesper cares enormously and learned early that feeling every patient's pain is unsurvivable, so curiosity became the safe way to care. Kai's magic is, to Vesper, the most important medical mystery alive — and the arc is Vesper realizing they want Kai *well*, not just *solved*.
- **With Kai:** he's simultaneously their favorite puzzle and, slowly, a person they'd be wrecked to lose. Romance-optional (the awkward-brilliant one who learns tenderness).

## Rurik — Warrior / Hunter *(new — the martial outlier)*
*axe or bow · rugged frontline · keeps the house from being all intellect*

- **Role:** Thornrook's muscle — a rangy woods-hunter who brings real melee/physicality to a house of thinkers.
- **Personality:** plainspoken, unpretentious, wilderness-raised from the western forests the house governs — more at home tracking game than in a library, and faintly amused by his bookish housemates. The house's pragmatism in its most literal, hands-dirty form.
- **Depth:** underestimated as "just the brawler," he's quietly the most *emotionally* intelligent person in Thornrook — reads people the way he reads a forest. Recognizes Kai the forager instantly as one of his own kind. Their shared woodcraft (Kai's whole origin is wandering the woods) is a lovely, specific bond.
- **With Kai:** two people who understand the forest and each other; grounded, warm, easy. Romance-optional (default platonic; a sturdy slow-burn if flagged).

## Teacher — Alaric, Keeper of the Old Records
*head of house · knows more about the wardstones than anyone alive*

- **Personality:** dry, patient, watchful — the archetypal old scholar, and the single most important adult NPC to the overarching mystery. As keeper of Thornrook's western archives, Alaric has read the fragments no one else connects (`WORLD_BIBLE.md` §3–4), and *strongly suspects* what Kai's arrival means. Doesn't tell him. Not out of malice — out of the scholar's caution that a half-understood truth handed to a frightened teenager does more harm than good.
- **The stake:** he is the campaign's slow-drip information source — the one who knows, or nearly knows, and doles it out on the story's schedule. His restraint is a real ethical question the story can interrogate: is protecting Kai from the truth the same as controlling him? Rina, his own heir, is on the path to the same knowledge, which makes their dynamic quietly loaded.
- **With Kai:** the mentor who holds the answer Kai most wants. Every conversation is a little bit of a chess game — Kai sensing there's more, Alaric deciding how much is safe. Their trust, if it forms, is the key that unlocks the mystery's middle act.
