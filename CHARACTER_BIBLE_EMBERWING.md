# CHARACTER_BIBLE_EMBERWING.md — House Emberwing

*Crimson & gold · Firebird · the southern reach · swords / cavalry / offense growths (STR/SPD)*
*Culture: bold, warm, heart-on-sleeve. Trust loudly, charge first, mean it.*

Companion to the other bibles. **Kael, Brenna, and Wren already exist as built units** (`MASTER_PLAN.md` STATUS) — their bios here are the character layer over the kit that's already tested; nothing about their mechanics needs to change. Perrin (heir) is in `CHARACTER_BIBLE_HEADS.md`. Roster coverage (heir + 5 students): cavalry/heir (Perrin), duelist (Kael), anchor (Brenna), healer (Wren), bruiser (Dain), battle-mage (Liora) — full functional spread from within the house's sword/cavalry/offense identity, with Liora supplying the house's only offensive range.

---

## Kael — Myrmidon → Swordmaster
*sword · fast · crit-leaning (built: WO-2.4 promotion, carries a Master Seal)*

- **Role:** glass-cannon duelist. High SPD/SKL, doubles and crits, folds if hit.
- **Personality:** easy, open, quick to laugh — the first person at Vael to treat Kai like a person instead of an incident, which is *why* Kai leans Emberwing (`CHARACTER_BIBLE_KAI.md`). No noble airs despite being house-born; would rather spar than talk politics. Loyal in the uncomplicated way that makes the complicated people trust him.
- **Depth:** the easy warmth is partly a choice — Kael watched the Academy's snobbery land on other scholarship-adjacent kids and decided early he wasn't going to be that. His lightness is a small daily act of defiance.
- **With Kai:** the natural first bond, not the mandated one. If the player invests elsewhere, Kael stays a good friend rather than *the* friend. Romance candidate (warm, low-angst, the "best friend you fall for").

## Brenna — Armored Knight
*lance · armored · slow/tanky (built unit — the Emberwing oddball)*

- **Role:** the wall. High DEF/HP, low SPD, holds chokes and eats hits. Unusual in an offense house — deliberately.
- **Personality:** steady, wry, unbothered. A noble who by every expectation should've gone to Tidemark's armored tradition, and chose Emberwing's fire instead — so she gets, from the inside, what it is to not fit your house's mold. That makes her Kai's natural confidante about being an outlier, from the opposite direction: she chose her misfit; his chose him.
- **Depth:** carries the house's boldness as *conviction* rather than noise — the quiet one who's actually the bravest, because she plants herself where it's worst and does not move.
- **With Kai:** older-sibling warmth, mutual outsider understanding. Romance-optional (leaning platonic-primary; a grounded slow-burn if flagged).

## Wren — Healer / Priest
*staff · no combat · gains XP from healing (built unit)*

- **Role:** the house's only dedicated healer. Keeps everyone standing; earns XP through the staff.
- **Personality:** soft-spoken, observant, allergic to the spotlight — which in a house of loud hearts makes her the still point everyone orbits. Notices what people need before they say it. The two people at Vael least interested in being looked at, her and Kai, recognize each other instantly.
- **Depth:** her calm is hard-won, not native — she heals because she's seen what goes unhealed, and there's a spine of steel under the gentleness that only shows when someone she's tending is threatened.
- **With Kai:** quiet mutual respect that deepens without ever needing to be loud. Romance candidate (the tender, understated one).

## Dain — Brawler / Fighter *(new — the house's fifth)*
*axe · high STR/HP, brash frontline bruiser · fills the pure-offense melee slot*

- **Role:** aggressive frontliner — big axe, big damage, big risks. The house's offensive identity at its most literal.
- **Personality:** loud, competitive, generous, a little insecure under the swagger — from a *minor* Emberwing house, low enough on the ladder that the great families barely register it (Emberwing cares least about pedigree, which is why he fits here). Sees Kai arrive to the whole Academy's fascination and feels a flash of "why him and not me," then is ashamed of it, then becomes fiercely protective — the arc from rivalry to brotherhood.
- **Depth:** the swagger covers a fear that without a *great name*, being the *strongest* is the only way to matter. Kai — who has no name at all and matters anyway — quietly dismantles that belief just by existing.
- **With Kai:** rival-to-brother, the game's most fun bickering support chain. Romance-optional (default bro-energy; can flag).

## Liora — Battle-Mage *(new — the house's sixth, and its only ranged attacker)*
*fire tome · aggressive close-range magic · MAG-offense glass cannon (distinct from Kael's physical crit)*

- **Role:** Emberwing's battle-mage and **only offensive range** — the house had zero ranged attacker without her, and this honors Emberwing's founding Warder line, which "fought — front-line battle-mages and blade-work" (`WORLD_BIBLE.md` §2). Fragile, hits hard at 1–2 range, wants to be aggressive.
- **Personality:** warm, passionate, impulsively generous — Emberwing's heart-on-sleeve fire, literally and temperamentally. Where the house's other loud hearts are physical (Perrin, Dain), hers is felt: she talks about magic like music, and can't stand to watch someone wrestle with power alone.
- **The Kai hook:** she's the one person at Vael who can help Kai understand what magic *feels like from the inside* — and being Emberwing, she doesn't study him or hesitate the way Thornrook would; she just grabs his hand and says "show me." A generous magic-peer with zero agenda — the warm counter to Isolde's envy and Corvin's specimen-curiosity. Structurally, she's the natural in-fiction support for Kai *practicing* his power as it wakes (complements, never replaces, the story-driven unlocks of M8).
- **Depth:** her generosity has a hidden cost — she pours into everyone else's fire and quietly neglects her own path, the passionate mentor who's a little lost about where she's going. Kai, who needs her help *and* actually notices her back, is the first to tend her flame in return.
- **With Kai:** the magic-bond — hands-on, warm, wondrous; the person who makes his terrifying power feel like a gift instead of a curse. Romance candidate (bright, generous, sees the magic as beautiful).

## Teacher — Callan, the Ember-Scarred
*veteran house mentor · not a standard playable unit (see note)*

- **Role in fiction:** Emberwing's head of house and combat instructor. Runs the Training Grounds lessons that double as tutorial flavor.
- **Personality:** grizzled, warm, blunt. Born to a landless, forgotten branch of a minor house — noble on paper, nothing in the bank — who earned a real battlefield commission through sheer merit before the peace. Everyone expected nothing of him; he became the best soldier of his generation anyway. That's precisely why he goes to bat for Kai's admission behind the scenes, and why he sees the kid coming a mile off. He already made every mistake Kai's about to make.
- **The scar / the stake:** carries an old wound and an old guilt — a command decision years ago where his own charge-first instinct got people killed. He teaches boldness *and* its price, and he's the living argument for Kai learning to aim his recklessness instead of drowning it. Quiet stake in the mystery: he's seen a wardstone "incident" before, out on campaign, and never got an answer for it — one of the few who won't dismiss what happened to Kai as a fluke.
- **With Kai:** mentor and the closest thing to a guide-who-gets-it. The underestimated man who made good, showing another how it's done — he knows what it is to be written off for an accident of birth.
- *Optional mechanic (parked): teachers as occasional guest/deployable units for special chapters, à la Three Houses. Fiction supports it; build only if wanted.*
