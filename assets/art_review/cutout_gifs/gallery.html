Emberline Tactics · battle direction v2

On-map attacks — flat sprite, swinging weapon

No battle popup, no rigged arm. The unit keeps its cheap 32×32 map sprite; a swappable weapon plays a motion matched to its TYPE while the whole sprite lunges with it. Camera pushes in on the clash.

Per-weapon motion, not one swing: the sword slashes, the lance stabs, the axe chops overhead. Kai carries his signature green blade (a unique drop); the axe swaps in from the shared weapon library. The base sprite is being stripped of its baked-in weapon so nothing doubles up.
