# VILLAIN_BIBLE.md — The Prince Who Died *(draft, spine v2 — 2026-07)*

> **THEME (locked 2026-07-09):** *Belonging isn't given by blood — it's built by what you protect.*


Companion to `WORLD_BIBLE.md` (§3, §3b, §4), `ACADEMY_BIBLE.md`, `CHARACTER_BIBLE_KAI.md`. The campaign's faced antagonist. **Draft for Neal to tear apart** — names and specifics are placeholders; the shape is the point.

> **Seeding rule:** he is a *pressure and a shadow* for a long time — agents, rumors, ripples — not a man who monologues in the prologue. Keep him mostly off-screen (a name, a hand behind events, at most a silhouette/epilogue seed) until the campaign has earned him.

## Who he is

- The **king's younger brother** — a prince of the modern crown, the visible remnant of the old royal line.
- Declared **dead** years ago: a disappearance / an expedition that never returned. The kingdom mourned and moved on. He is not dead.
- Where the court treats the **First Crown** as legend, he *believed* it — and has spent his presumed-death years **searching the world** for the true source of the ancient royal power, to take it for himself and become what the royal line used to be.
- **Placeholder name/title.** Leave open unless the docs settle a naming pattern (nobles go by given names; the crown may use a house/regnal name). Don't over-lock.

## What drives him

- He wants the **First Crown's power** — not a throne he could already scheme for, but the *real* thing the throne is a faded shadow of.
- He has earned it, in his own mind: years of exile, study, and sacrifice chasing what everyone else forgot.
- And now the source has finally **answered** — not to him, after all of it, but to **a forager's son from Oakstead who wandered into a cave.** That fury — *a commoner has my birthright* — is his engine. Kai is simultaneously the thing he hates and the key he needs.
- He is not a cackler. He should read as someone who believes he is *owed*, and is coldly, patiently certain he's right.

## What he knows vs. only suspects

- **Knows:** the First Crown is real; there are sanctums; the power can be reclaimed by the right blood; something at the source has stirred (the awakening was loud).
- **Knows / suspects about Kai:** that *someone* triggered a sanctum. How much he knows about Kai specifically — that he's of the line, a lost heir — is a dial to turn as the campaign wants. Good version: he learns it before Kai does, which is its own horror.
- **May know more than Kai** about **how Kai's line survived** to a foundling in Oakstead (a hidden heir, a cadet branch, an infant smuggled away). This is a reveal to hold.
- **Doesn't know / can't control:** that the relic answers Kai and not him; that Kai's instinct to protect the powerless is exactly what the prince lacks.

## His reach (agents, not himself)

- **Bandits / mercenaries / cult-of-the-old-power** as deniable hands. The prologue road raid can be the first, deniable ripple of his search (`CAMPAIGN_STRUCTURE.md`) — looks random, isn't.
- Someone inside or adjacent to the court and/or the Academy who feeds him information is plausible and useful later; don't lock it yet.
- Connection to the **First Crown / ancient line / the court** is the spine of his threat — he is *family*, which is what makes him dangerous and tragic rather than a generic dark lord.

## Tone & role

- A **mirror to Kai**: same blood, opposite answer to it. Kai reclaims an inheritance he never asked for and instinctively protects the powerless; the prince spent his life demanding an inheritance and would burn the powerless to get it.
- Escalates across the campaign from *rumor* → *agents* → *silhouette* → *the man himself* at the back half (`CAMPAIGN_STRUCTURE.md` act 6).
