# TOWN_OF_VAEL.md — the city outside the gates (M10)

*Design doc (Fable draft for Neal, 2026-07-09). Canon: MASTER_PLAN M10 +
WORLD_BIBLE — Vael is the crown-land town in the Vael Reach; the **King of
Aldreth's castle** stands here (and the king's "dead" brother is the campaign's
villain — the castle is a story anchor, not set dressing). The campus
gatehouse's south road is the entrance.*

## 1. Layout — hub-and-spoke, campus toolkit

Same architecture that fixed the campus: a grand **Town Square** hub whose
gates open onto themed districts. All rooms run on the castle-v2 system
(CastleScene), so doors/NPCs/points/music/editor all just work.

| Room | What's there |
|---|---|
| **vael_square** (hub) | grand fountain, the king's statue, notice board, townsfolk walkers, shopfront facades; gates to everything |
| **vael_market** | smithy + general goods + the **arcane bookshop** (three working shops), market stalls |
| **vael_castle_gate** | the King's castle, massive, gates sealed (story-gated — opens when the plot says so); royal guards |
| **vael_cathedral** | the Grand Chantry (faith is a school of magic) — candle-lighting, a priest, choir later |
| **vael_riverside** | the docks: the **Gilded Stag tavern** (meals with housemates = bond), fishing pier, moored boats, the playhouse |

## 2. The economy (finally, something to spend gold on)

Gold sources already live: arena winnings, quiz commendations, report-card
chests. Sinks:

- **Smithy** — iron weapons; a steel tier later.
- **General goods** — vulneraries, tonics.
- **Arcane bookshop** — **SPELLBOOKS** (SPELLBOOKS.md route 3): very expensive
  (~900g), one per school, Kai-only; buying one opens the school + grants the
  tome, same as acing an exam. The chase purchase.
- **Tavern meal** — cheap (20g), pays out in bond instead of items.

## 3. Fun places for students (Neal's ask) — research notes

Pulled from the games that do "school town" best:

- **FE: Three Houses (monastery)** — shared meals raise support; a marketplace
  with smith/gifts; fishing; lost items. → **Adopt: tavern meals (built now),
  gift-giving (buy a gift, give it for bond — build next), fishing pier.**
- **Persona** — hangout spots that level relationships; arcade minigames; jobs.
  → **Adopt: the playhouse + games parlor as date spots (facades now, scenes
  at the co-write); maybe odd jobs later for gold.**
- **Stardew Valley** — seasonal festivals in the town square. → **Adopt: the
  calendar's festival weeks can MOVE to the square (decorated variants).**
- **Bully / Fable** — shops that react to reputation. → **Later: prices/stock
  react to the semester GRADE (an S-rank discount, "the academy's star").**

## 4. Story hooks planted (not written — co-write)

- The **castle gates are sealed** — guards turn students away. The first time
  the king summons Kai (mid-campaign), the gates opening IS the beat.
- The **cathedral** half-remembers the First Crown as scripture ("the Giver of
  Flame"). Alaric hates their translation. Lore shelves → quiz questions.
- The **notice board** is the future side-quest surface.
- Townsfolk gossip = drip-feed for the dead-prince rumors.
